## 🌌 About Adapt OS

**Adapt OS** is an adaptive digital workspace designed to bring productivity, learning, creativity, organization, and personal development together.

Created by **Emmanuel Pobee Okpoti** in Accra, Ghana, Adapt OS is built around one central idea:

> **Technology should adapt to the user, not the other way around.**

Instead of forcing everyone into the same workflow, Adapt OS aims to provide a flexible environment where users can organize their work, develop their skills, explore ideas, and build meaningful projects.

The **Adapt OS Major Edition** is the main development branch of the project.

## ✨ Explore the Workspace

Adapt OS is designed around a flexible workspace concept.

### 📚 Learning & Academic Productivity

Organize academic activities and develop skills through dedicated learning tools.

### 🗂️ Organization & Planning

Bring tasks, notes, files, and planning workflows into a more organized digital environment.

### 🔬 Research & Discovery

Explore information, investigate topics, and develop ideas through research-oriented workflows.

### 💻 Development & Creativity

Create, experiment, and build projects in a workspace designed to support creative thinking.

### 📊 Productivity Insights

Use structured information and productivity insights to better understand your work.

> **Development note:** These are the project's intended capability areas. Availability depends on the features implemented in the current version.
